Four directions for the interactive piece

All on the palette from :5320, all grained. The test each one has to pass: it has to mean something. Yichen's blobs bounce because bouncing looks nice — that is the thing not to copy.

1 · The lens

A real refracting disc: concentric rings sampled at different scales give barrel distortion, and the rim splits into colour fringes. Drag it over the type and the words bend.

Drag · lens   Release · settle
You design for a display that works by bending light. The dispersion is already in your HUD's glass.css.

2 · Ink bloom

Pigment spreads into wet paper and keeps creeping after you let go. Overlaps mix subtractively, so two washes go deeper rather than brighter — the way paint does, not light.

Drag · paint   Hold · flood
You came to design through painting. This makes the visitor do it.

3 · Graphite

Was a long exposure. A long exposure is light on darkness, so it did not survive the ground being locked to paper. The mechanic did: a surface that accumulates, where passing twice is darker than passing once. On paper that is a pencil, and paper never forgets.

Move · draw   Cross · press
Now the closest of the four to "she came up as an artist", and unlike the wash it never becomes a mess.

4 · Track Record, earned by the visit

Same subtractive tissue stack as your piece, but a disc is stamped when you do something rather than when a runner closes a lap. Position comes from where you were when you earned it, so the composition is a record of this visit.

Click · stamp   Wander · drift
Keeps the argument that makes your piece good — the picture cannot exist until the work is done — and hands the work to the reader.
